# Photo credits

Photographs are real images from India, used under their free licences. Each is shown in the app with its credit line, and is loaded from Wikimedia's image servers (nothing is modified). To use your own plant photos instead, put them in `public/photos/` and point the matching entry in `src/lib/photos.ts` at `/photos/<file>`.
